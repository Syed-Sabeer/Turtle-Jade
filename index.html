Our Story

A house built on family tides.

Turtles by Finley Jade began the way most good stories do — around a family table, sun-worn from a week at the shore, polos stiff with salt and easy afternoons. What started as an eye for the way light moves across water became a philosophy for how resort wear should feel: unhurried, considered, built for the years between vacations, not just the week of one.

Every piece carries the palette of the tide and the quiet confidence of clothes made to become heirlooms, not costumes.

The Collections

Five ways to go the distance.

Each collection is a chapter of the same idea — pieces made for the long run, named for the moments and waters that shape a coastal life.

01 — Long Weekend

The Long Weekend Collection

Friday to Monday, made easy.

The Long Weekend is the stretch of days that feels like a small holiday — the kind where time slows down and the only agenda is the tide.

These pieces are built for that ease: considered enough for a dinner that runs late, relaxed enough for a morning that doesn't start in a hurry. Made to be reached for from Friday to Monday, and long after.

02 — Returning Tide

The Returning Tide Collection

What goes out always comes back.

The tide always returns. So do the things worth keeping — the people, the places, the garments that earn their place over the years.

The Returning Tide is for the pieces that come back into rotation season after season, never falling out of favor. Quiet, dependable, and made to endure.

Palette — Deep Navy
03 — First Light

The First Light Collection

For the early hours on the water.

First light is the quietest, most honest part of the day — when the water is still and the world hasn't yet caught up.

The First Light Collection is built for those early hours: light, breathable, and unfussy. Pieces that move with you from the first cast to the first coffee.

Palette — Morning Tide
04 — The Passage

The Passage Collection

For the journeys in between.

Every passage is the space between where you were and where you're going — the crossings, the flights, the long quiet stretches of open water.

The Passage Collection is made for the in-between: garments that travel well, wrinkle little, and look considered on either side of the journey.

05 — Home Waters

The Home Waters Collection

The places we return to.

Home waters are the shores we know best — the familiar bays, the local docks, the coastline that feels like it belongs to us.

The Home Waters Collection honors the idea of return: pieces rooted in place, made for the days you spend closest to home and the people you share them with.

Before you order

Everything you need to know.

Fit, delivery, exchanges, and care—answered simply.

Shop the Polo
How does the Long Run Polo fit?

It has a classic tailored fit: comfortable through the chest and shoulders with a clean, relaxed line through the waist. Choose your usual polo size; size up if you prefer a looser resort fit.

When will my order arrive?

Orders are prepared within one to two business days. Standard delivery typically arrives within three to five business days after dispatch, with tracking provided by email.

Can I exchange the size?

Yes. Unworn polos with their original tags may be exchanged within 30 days. The limited edition number remains tied to the original garment and cannot be reserved during an exchange.

How should I care for the piqué?

Machine wash cold with similar colors, reshape the collar while damp, and lay flat or tumble dry low. Avoid bleach and high heat to protect the fabric and embroidered crest.

Our Story

A house built on family tides.

Turtles by Finley Jade began the way most good stories do — around a family table, sun-worn from a week at the shore, polos stiff with salt and easy afternoons. What started as an eye for the way light moves across water became a philosophy for how resort wear should feel: unhurried, considered, built for the years between vacations, not just the week of one.

Every piece we cut carries that same throughline — the palette of the tide, the ease of a long lunch, the quiet confidence of clothes that don't ask to be noticed until you're wearing them. This is resort wear as heirloom, not costume.

Craftsmanship

The details we don't compromise on.

Piqué, Chosen With Intent

A double-knit piqué that breathes like linen and holds its shape through salt, sun, and the hundredth wash.

A Collar Built to Hold

Fused interlining, rolled by hand and set with a double row of stitching — structure that lasts for years, not weeks.

Embroidered, Never Printed

Every turtle is laid down in dense, high-tension thread — a small detail, done slowly.

Numbered, Then Closed

Each polo is numbered within its run of 200. A garment with a serial, not a season.

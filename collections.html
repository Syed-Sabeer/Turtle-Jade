The Long Weekend

Friday to Monday, made easy.

The long weekend is the stretch of days that feels like a small holiday — the kind where time slows down and the only agenda is the tide.

These are pieces built for that ease: considered enough for a dinner that runs late, relaxed enough for a morning that doesn’t start in a hurry. Made to be reached for from Friday to Monday, and long after.

The Returning Tide

What goes out always comes back.

The tide always returns. So do the things worth keeping — the people, the places, the garments that earn their place over the years.

The Returning Tide is for the pieces that come back into rotation season after season and never fall out of favour. Quiet, dependable, made to endure.

First Light

For the early hours on the water.

First light is the quietest, most honest part of the day — when the water is still and the world hasn’t caught up yet.

First Light is built for those hours: light, breathable and unfussy. Pieces that move with you from the first cast to the first coffee.

The Passage

For the journeys in between.

Every passage is the space between where you were and where you are going — the crossings, the flights, the long quiet stretches of open water.

The Passage is made for the in-between: garments that travel well, wrinkle little, and look considered on either side of the journey.

Home Waters

The places we return to.

Home waters are the shores you know best — the familiar bays, the local docks, the coastline that feels like it belongs to you.

Home Waters honours the idea of return: pieces rooted in place, made for the days spent closest to home and the people you share them with.

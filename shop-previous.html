Find your size, once.

Before you order

Everything you need to know.

Fit, delivery, exchanges, and care — answered simply.

Back to Shop
How does the Long Run Polo fit?

It has a classic tailored fit: comfortable through the chest and shoulders with a clean, relaxed line through the waist. Choose your usual polo size; size up if you prefer a looser resort fit. See the size chart above for exact measurements.

When will my order arrive?

Orders are prepared within one to two business days. Standard delivery typically arrives within three to five business days after dispatch, with tracking provided by email.

Do you ship outside the U.S.?

At the moment we ship within the United States only, with complimentary shipping on every order. International delivery is on our roadmap — join the Clubhouse below to hear first when it opens.

Can I exchange the size?

Yes. Unworn polos with their original tags may be exchanged within 30 days. The limited edition number remains tied to the original garment and cannot be reserved during an exchange.

How should I care for the piqué?

Machine wash cold with similar colors, reshape the collar while damp, and lay flat or tumble dry low. Avoid bleach and high heat to protect the fabric and embroidered crest.

What happens when a colorway sells out?

Each colorway is a closed run of 200 — once it sells through, it will not be restocked. New colorways are announced through the Clubhouse before they appear on the shop.
